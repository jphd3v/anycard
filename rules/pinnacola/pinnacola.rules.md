## Overview

Pinnacola (Pinella-style partnership variant) is played with **four players in two teams** using **two standard 52-card decks plus jokers** (108 cards total). The rules are mostly based on the [Italian Wikipedia article on Pinnacola](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinnacola). The objective is for a **team** to be the first to reach **1000 points**. Points are scored by forming melds (runs and sets) and collecting bonuses. A hand ends when a player **goes out by discarding the last card**.

## Setup

- **Players**: 4 players, two teams (partners sit opposite).
- **Dealing**: Each player is dealt **19 cards**.
- **Stock**: The remaining cards form a face-down stock.
- **Discard (Pozzo)**: One card is flipped face-up to start the discard. Discards are kept **visible in order** (typically fanned/stacked so the order is known).
- **Wild Cards**:
  - **Jokers** are wild and may substitute for any missing card in a meld.
  - **Black twos (2♠, 2♣)** are also wild ("pinelle/mattini") but have **special movement restrictions** (see below).

## Turn Structure

Each turn consists of three phases:

### Phase 1: The Draw

Exactly one draw is taken from one of the following sources:

- **Draw from stock**: The top card is taken from the face-down stock.
- **Take from the discard (take _n_)**: One or more cards may be taken from the discard, **provided that the deepest (bottommost/oldest) card taken can be melded immediately** in Phase 2.
  - When a discard pickup is performed, the selected deepest card and **all cards discarded after it** are taken into the hand.
  - If the selected deepest card cannot be melded immediately, the pickup is not permitted and the draw must be from stock instead.

### Phase 2: Melding

During the melding phase, any combination of the following may be performed, in any order:

- New melds may be laid onto the table for the player’s **team**.

- Cards may be added to existing melds belonging to the player’s **team**.

- **Joker replacement (mobile joker rule)**:
  - A Joker currently used in a meld is permitted to be replaced with the natural card it represents.
  - If a Joker is replaced, it is required to be **reused immediately** in a meld (which allows it to effectively move between melds).

- **Black two (“pinella/mattino”) movement restriction**:
  - A black two used as a wild card **may not be removed from the meld it is in** (it cannot be moved to another meld).
  - A black two is permitted to **change position within the same meld**, provided the meld remains valid.

### Phase 3: The Discard

The turn ends by discarding **exactly one card** to the discard.

- **Going out**: The last card in hand is permitted to be discarded to end the hand (no separate minimum-meld requirement is assumed in this variant).

## Melding Rules

- **Runs (Scala)**: 3+ cards of the same suit in sequence.
- **Sets (Tris)**: 3 cards of the same rank with different suits.
- **Poker** (for bonus purposes): 4 cards of the same rank (one of each suit), **natural-only** (no wild cards).
- **Pinnacola** (for bonus purposes): a run of **7 or more** cards in the same suit, typically treated as **clean (natural-only)** when awarding doubling bonuses.
- **Pinnacolone / Reale**: a complete run from Ace through King in a single suit (13 cards), **clean (natural-only)**.

Wild cards (Jokers and black twos) may be used in ordinary runs/sets as substitutes, subject to the movement rules above. Whether a meld containing wild cards qualifies as "clean" for doubling/tripling is handled by the table's scoring conventions below.

## Scoring

The hand score for each team is calculated as:

**(Points in the team’s melded cards + bonuses) − (Points of cards remaining in that team’s hands)**

### Card Values

- **Joker**: 30 pts
- **Black two used as wild (“pinella/mattino”)**: 20 pts
- **Ace**: 15 pts
- **King, Queen, Jack, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6**: 10 pts each
- **5, 4, 3, 2**: 5 pts each
  - (If a 2 is being used as a _pinella/mattino_ wild, it uses the 20-pt value above.)

### Bonuses

- **Closing (going out)**: +100 pts.

**Four-of-a-kind (“Poker”) bonuses (natural-only poker):**

- **Poker of 2–5**: +40 pts
- **Poker of 6–K**: +80 pts
- **Poker of Aces**: +120 pts
- **Poker of Jokers (4 jokers)**: +500 pts

**Run multipliers (clean runs):**

- If at least one **clean pinnacola** (7+ run without jokers) is present on the table, **all card values scored in melds are doubled**.
- In some tables, if a clean run of **10+** cards exists, **all card values scored in melds are tripled** instead of doubled.

**Pinnacolone / Reale (A-to-K clean run):**

- **+1500 pts**, and the match is typically considered **immediately won** when this is achieved.

### Match Target

- The match is played until a team reaches **1000 points** (table target for this variant).

## Game Strategy

- **Protect your jokers**—they're worth 30 points each and highly flexible, but if left in your hand when someone goes out, they become a major penalty.
- **Think twice before using black twos**—once placed in a meld, black twos (pinelle) cannot be moved to another meld, only repositioned within the same meld.
- **Aim for a clean pinnacola early**—a 7+ card run without wild cards doubles all your meld values, making it one of the most powerful bonuses in the game.
- **Watch opponent melds**—avoid discarding cards that help opponents extend their runs or complete sets, especially if they're close to a pinnacola.
- **Calculate discard pile value**—taking multiple cards from the discard can be powerful, but only if the deepest card you take can be melded immediately.
- **Balance going out with score maximization**—going out gives +100 points, but sometimes it's worth waiting to build more melds or complete a pinnacola for the doubling bonus.
